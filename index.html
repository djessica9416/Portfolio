RiseAgain, an AI-powered career platform

Category: 0-1 Product Development Founder and Product Manager, 2026–present A working MVP that matches resumes to job descriptions and shows candidates how to close the gaps.
Problem
Job seekers juggle separate tools for discovery, tracking, resume edits, and interview prep, and rarely know why they aren’t getting responses.
My role
Solo founder: vision, roadmap, MVP scope, and hands-on build using AI-assisted development tools.
Approach
  • Built three personas, empathy maps, and a job-seeker journey map to find where people get stuck, and to define what to test with interviews.
  • Turned user pain points into prioritized requirements across job discovery, application tracking, resume optimization, and interview prep.
  • Designed a resume-to-job-description matching experience that flags compatibility gaps and recommends actions.
  • Integrated API-connected AI features into working application flows and assessed technical feasibility along the way.
Result
A functional MVP that moved from concept to working workflows. Next step: validating assumptions with users.

Discovery research for Expedia and Vrbo

Category: User Research Expedia Group, Customer Experience Feedback Pod, 2021–2022 Research with 100+ users became three backlog items, released within two development cycles.
Problem
Guests on the Vrbo marketplace kept running into the same issues, and they surfaced as repeat claims and support cases.
My role
Representative of the Americas Guarantee Claims team, working directly with Product Managers and Engineering.
Approach
  • Ran discovery research with 100+ Expedia and Vrbo users.
  • Narrowed the findings to three high-impact pain points.
  • Wrote them up as product requirements and helped refine and prioritize the backlog.
  • Supported launches and post-release evaluation to confirm the fixes addressed root causes.
Result
All three pain points shipped within two development cycles. Research also fed the platform’s accessibility search filters (wheelchair access, single-level properties, entrance ramps) and helped reduce rebooking-related support cases.

How I work

The habits behind the case studies.
